# Electric Vehicle Charging Point Navigator

As the name itself suggests, this app is a peer-to-peer electric charging point navigator. India has a big scarcity of electric charging stations, especially outside big cities and if you want to travel long distances on your EV, you have to be vary of the charging stations on the way which many of the times might not exist and you might have to take detour or a longer route to go through a path charging station is there.

But what if someone with an EV lives near the area where you want to charge your vehicle. That's exactly what we want to make accessible through this app. 

So basically this app acts as an interface between 2 EV owners, one who is traveling and one who is at home acting as a charging point since he would already have an EV charger at his home. Of course, there are lot of technicalities involved on the car company, charger company, charger type, etc. which we try to solve through this app
